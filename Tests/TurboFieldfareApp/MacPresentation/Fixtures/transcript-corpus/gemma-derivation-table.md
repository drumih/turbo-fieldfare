### 1. Derivation of the Quadratic Formula

To solve $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ for $x$, we use the method of **completing the square**.

**Step 1: Isolate the $x$ terms**
Subtract $c$ from both sides:
$$ax^2 + bx = -c$$

**Step 2: Divide by $a$**
To make the leading coefficient 1:
$$x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x = -\frac{c}{a}$$

**Step 3: Complete the square**
Take half of the coefficient of $x$ (which is $\frac{b}{2a}$), square it ($\frac{b^2}{4a^2}$), and add it to both sides:
$$x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x + \frac{b^2}{4a^2} = \frac{b^2}{4a^2} - \frac{c}{a}$$

**Step 4: Factor and simplify**
The left side is now a perfect square. On the right side, find a common denominator ($4a^2$):
$$\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2}$$

**Step 5: Take the square root**
Take the square root of both sides, remembering to include $\pm$:
$$x + \frac{b}{2a} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2}}$$
$$x + \frac{b}{2a} = \pm \frac{\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

**Step 6: Solve for $x$**
Subtract $\frac{b}{2a}$ from both sides:
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

### 2. Cosine Similarity Formula
Cosine similarity measures the cosine of the angle between two non-zero vectors. It is calculated as:
$$\text{similarity}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}) = \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}}{\|\mathbf{A}\| \|\mathbf{B}\|} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i B_i}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i^2} \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} B_i^2}}$$
